What comes with every quote

Routine quoting against federal clauses, with the documentation your file needs already attached.

  • 01

    Line-item pricing

    Quotes and completed forms returned within posted deadlines.

  • 02

    Buy American Act

    Compliant product lines available across all categories, certified per product.

  • 03

    Country of origin

    Manufacturer-attested statements. TAA-designated origins verified per FAR Part 25 / DFARS 252.225-7001.

  • 04

    Engineering stamps

    Wind and snow load stamps for shade and shelter products.

  • 05

    IUID marking

    Available per MIL-STD-130 when the contract requires it.

  • 06

    The paperwork

    W-9, SAM.gov confirmation, insurance and resale certificates, cut sheets and warranties.
